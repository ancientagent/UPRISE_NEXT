# UPRISE App-Side Crew

This operational room map points to product authority under `docs/specs/**`.
Rooms own lanes and artifacts, not final product truth.

| Seat | Owns | Does not own | Reports to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `🟣 UPRISE • Manager • <state>` | packets, sequencing, writer lease, cross-lane reconciliation, disposition | routine specialist work or implementation | founder / Cross-Project PM |
| `🟣 UPRISE • Context Steward • <state>` | documentation authority, owner-contract routing, handoff promotion, stale-doc cleanup | product decisions or implementation | Manager |
| `🟣 UPRISE • Landing Page + Launch Entry • <state>` | one public launch surface and its design package | other screens or platform doctrine | Manager |
| `🟣 UPRISE • Marketing + Community Growth • <state>` | campaign/launch roadmap, positioning, landing content brief, media kit/content, artist/source/community outreach, channels, measurement proposals | product roadmap, unapproved promises/pricing, visual/frontend implementation | Manager |

Marketing supplies an approved brief to the relevant design surface; it does
not absorb that design room. Product claims derive from owner specs and founder
locks. The Manager approves priority, timing, promises, and disposition.
