# UPRISE Tower Descent Storyboard

Status: founder-approved visual direction, August 1, 2026

These are design-department keyframes, not shipped product assets.

## Camera Sequence

1. `01-establishing-height.png` - crane-height city establishing frame; the
   unfinished tower is centered while surrounding building edges create depth.
2. `02-tower-spine-descent.png` - the camera moves straight down the tower's
   central spine, passing crossbeams, scaffolding, and a few worker figures.
3. `03-concrete-threshold.png` - the camera reaches the tower footing and
   passes through cracked concrete into brief black xerox grain.
4. `04-underground-laptop-reveal.png` - the camera emerges behind a couch in a
   small underground DIY room; a laptop in the seated listener's lap is framed
   against the tiny stage about ten feet away before the screen takes over.

## Direction Locks

- One continuous vertical camera route, centered on the tower, with no alley
  detour or stacked web-page sections.
- Surrounding city buildings are tight, ambiguous, and parallax layers rather
  than a detailed skyline subject.
- The room is compact and shadowy, not a large bunker or venue.
- The laptop is a worn portable machine with a dark amber/olive terminal;
  there is no freestanding oversized monitor.
- Outside the laptop: xerox black, paper, and charcoal. Inside the laptop:
  muted amber/olive screen treatment. The bright green circle in
  `02-tower-spine-descent.png` is an unwanted generator artifact, not a color
  direction.
