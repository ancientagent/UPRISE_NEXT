## Brief

Create twelve male-presenting torso/outfit modules using one fixed neck socket,
shoulder registration, centerline, scale, and belly crop. Clothing may create
different apparent builds, but the connection geometry remains compatible.

Represent a useful range of everyday, punk, skin, crust, street, mod, and
folk-punk wardrobe without turning subcultures into costumes or stereotypes.
Keep all outerwear open and preserve readable undershirt merchandise zones.

The sheet includes:

1. worn band tee;
2. layered short- and long-sleeve tee;
3. pullover hoodie;
4. open zip hoodie;
5. open denim vest;
6. open moto jacket;
7. open patched canvas vest;
8. open flannel;
9. polo with braces;
10. open work jacket over polo;
11. open cardigan;
12. open folk/work overshirt.

Use bold, simplified hand-inked comic linework; black, off-white, and gray;
and one restrained fluorescent yellow-green accent. Avoid brand marks, real
band names, slogans, symbols of hate, bare anatomy, photorealism, or paper-doll
tabs.

## Approval Boundary

Founder approval selects the clothing vocabulary, silhouette range, line
character, and fixed-registration direction. Approved modules still require
manual redraw and cleanup, transparent layer separation, masks, anchor
coordinates, attachment zones, manifests, and crop QA.
