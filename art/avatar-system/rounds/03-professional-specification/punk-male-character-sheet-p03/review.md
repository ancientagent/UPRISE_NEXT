# Punk Male Character Sheet P03 Review

Status: retained revision reference; improved direction
Date: 2026-07-26

## Review Asset

- `01-punk-male-character-sheet-p03.png`

## What Worked

- Sixteen male-presenting Punk characters use a consistent belly-up grid and
  remain recognizably connected to the approved R3 illustration balance.
- Faces, expressions, skin tones, hair, and garments vary enough to avoid one
  repeated character wearing different clothes.
- Open outerwear consistently preserves the undershirt display area.
- Vest, jacket, hoodie, flannel, and overshirt constructions now include
  visibly reserved front patch panels.
- Primary patch panels are more legible than the dense lapel treatment in the
  earlier character sheet.
- Accent color remains restrained and useful for hair, shirts, pins, and
  simple patch marks.

## Review Risks

- Founder feedback confirms the models are better, but the roster needs more
  spikes and related Punk garment/hair energy.
- Several patch panels are tall and narrow.
- Small ear jewelry still appears frequently even though large hoops were
  reduced; accessory density needs explicit founder review.
- Devilock, bihawk, trihawk, and rat-tail silhouettes are not all clearly
  demonstrated in this full-character sheet.
- Some test patches are shown already installed. Production art must keep the
  patch object separate from the garment host zone.
- Generated text and symbols remain visual placeholders until manually redrawn
  and rights/provenance checked.
- Empty outlined patch placeholders are unnecessary in the next character
  sheet. Show installed fictional band-name or logo patches so the review
  proves usable scale without mistaking the patch for garment anatomy.

## Next Pass

- Increase varied spiked hair, studded collars, shoulder spikes, and other
  researched Punk details without turning every character into the same
  silhouette.
- Replace blank patch rectangles with separate installed sample patches using
  short fictional UPRISE band names or simple owned marks.
- Preserve quiet host panels underneath those sample patches and keep
  undershirt art visible.

## Approval Boundary

Approval may select the character balance, garment proportions, and patch-panel
construction. Every selected model still requires manual redraw, transparent
layer separation, shared registration, hair-color layers, accessory isolation,
garment manifests, and crop QA before development use.
