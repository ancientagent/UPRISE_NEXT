# Punk Head And Hair Presets P01 Mixed

Date: 2026-07-24

## Purpose

Test whether 20 head, face, expression, and hairstyle presets could use the
approved R3 illustration balance, a fixed neck baseline, and one neutral
recolorable skin-mask region.

## Key Constraints

- Exactly 20 head-and-hair identity presets.
- Same scale, crop, neck width, center, and lower cut.
- No clothing or shoulders.
- Neutral skin-mask fill instead of separate tone duplicates.
- Strong expression and hairstyle variation.
- No photorealism or portrait-level facial detail.
