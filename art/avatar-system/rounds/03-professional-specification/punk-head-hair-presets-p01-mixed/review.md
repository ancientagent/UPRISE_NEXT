# Punk Head And Hair Presets P01 Mixed Review

Status: revision reference; presentation sequence and research insufficient
Date: 2026-07-24

## Review Asset

- `01-punk-head-hair-presets-p01-mixed.png`

## What Worked

- The simplified R3 rendering balance remained recognizable.
- Twenty readable head-and-hair identities fit one sheet.
- Neutral face, ear, and neck regions demonstrate the separate skin-mask idea.
- The neck cuts are substantially more consistent than earlier head studies.
- The sheet remains a viable reference for the overall head-and-hair balance;
  it is not rejected as a visual direction.

## Revision Required

- The sheet mixed masculine, feminine, and ambiguous presentations instead of
  following the founder-selected sequence.
- The hairstyle range relied too heavily on generic mohawks, spikes,
  undercuts, shags, and bobs.
- It did not reflect enough period, regional, or subcommunity research.
- It omitted the devilock and several other important historical silhouettes.
- Hoop earrings repeat across too many identities and read as a default
  character trait instead of an optional accessory.
