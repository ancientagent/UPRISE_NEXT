## Brief

Create one landscape review sheet containing eight front-facing,
male-presenting bust/outfit modules. Every module uses the same centered neck
socket, shoulder baseline, scale, and belly crop. Show no heads, faces, hair,
hands, bare chests, or gendered anatomy.

Use the classic UPRISE Plot/Home Scene illustration direction: bold black
hand-inked comic linework, simplified forms, slight DIY texture, off-white and
grayscale fields, and one restrained fluorescent yellow-green accent.

Include:

1. black band tee with a readable chest-print zone;
2. off-white graphic tee;
3. pullover hoodie;
4. open sleeveless denim vest over a band tee;
5. open leather moto jacket over a band tee;
6. open plaid overshirt over a band tee;
7. white polo shirt;
8. tee with separate suspenders.

Outerwear remains open. Shirt art stays visible. Patches and buttons appear
only as attachment-zone demonstrations, not baked collectible assets.

## Approval Boundary

Approval selects the garment silhouette, line quality, crop, and shared
registration direction. It does not approve the generated sheet as final
layered artwork. Selected modules still require redraw/cleanup, transparent
separation, fixed anchors, masks, attachment zones, manifests, and crop QA.
