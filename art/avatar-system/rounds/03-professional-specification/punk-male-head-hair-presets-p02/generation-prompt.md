# Punk Male Head And Hair Presets P02

Date: 2026-07-24

## Purpose

Create the first researched male-presenting Punk identity pack after the mixed
P01 sheet failed the founder-selected presentation sequence and hairstyle
research threshold.

## Source Research

- `../../../research/2026-07-24_punk-hair-scene-atmosphere-research.md`
- `../stylized-punk-character-sheet-r3/01-stylized-punk-character-sheet-r3.png`
- `../../../references/legacy-avatar-boards/0_3 (1).jpeg`

## Required Output

- One landscape 6-by-4 sheet.
- Exactly 24 male-presenting head-and-hair identity presets.
- Same head scale, neutral skin-mask fill, neck width, center, and lower cut.
- No clothing, shoulders, hats, UI, construction diagrams, or tone duplicates.
- Preserve the founder-approved R3 detail balance.

## Required Hair Vocabulary

- Early short/chopped 1977 treatments.
- Fan mohawk, liberty spikes, and segmented spike hawk.
- Deathhawk, devilock, and psychobilly quiff.
- Hardcore crew cut, shaved head, and close working-class crop.
- Skate shag and jagged DIY mullet.
- Loc, natural-curl, braid, and uneven long-hair constructions.
- Day-glo charged spikes.
- Bihawk, trihawk, rat-tail crop, and reverse mohawk.

## Authenticity Rules

- Hair may be self-cut, hand-dyed, grown out, imperfect, or practical.
- Do not make every identity a giant mohawk or a salon-finished fashion model.
- Do not infer politics, class, race, sexuality, addiction, or personality
  from a hairstyle.
- Do not copy famous faces, real band logos, or documentary photographs.
