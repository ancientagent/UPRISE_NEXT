# Punk Male Head And Hair Presets P02 Review

Status: pending founder review
Date: 2026-07-24

## Review Asset

- `01-punk-male-head-hair-presets-p02.png`

## Current QA

- The sheet contains 24 male-presenting identities in a 6-by-4 grid.
- Skin areas use one neutral maskable treatment rather than tone duplicates.
- Neck cuts are substantially normalized.
- The sheet includes early short Punk, mohawk, liberty-spike, deathrock,
  horror-punk, psychobilly, hardcore, skate, loc, braid, curl, bihawk,
  trihawk, rat-tail, and reverse-mohawk directions.
- Expressions vary across serious, relaxed, amused, surprised, smiling, and
  confrontational states.
- The result remains concept art rather than production-separated assets.

## Review Risks

- The devilock may need a longer, more unmistakable forward point.
- The bihawk and trihawk must remain readable when reduced to avatar scale.
- Several quiet short-hair identities may need stronger distinction without
  becoming caricatures.
- The rat tail needs side or three-quarter visibility while preserving the
  fixed neck anchor.

## Founder Review

- Is this male-only sequencing now correct?
- Which hairstyles are strong enough to retain?
- Which historical or subcommunity hair directions are still missing?
- Does any identity remain too generic, too detailed, or too similar to
  another?
- Should the devilock, bihawk, trihawk, or rat tail receive dedicated revision
  studies before Pack 02?

## Approval Boundary

Approval selects identities and hair directions for deterministic redraw. It
does not approve the generated raster sheet as production-ready artwork.
