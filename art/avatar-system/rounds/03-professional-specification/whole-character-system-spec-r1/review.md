# Whole-Character Catalog System Specification R1 Review

Status: retained as an unrequested architecture exploration
Date: 2026-07-24

## Review Asset

- `01-whole-character-system-spec-r1.png`

## What This Sheet Tests

- Whether whole-character selection is clear enough to replace exposed
  paper-doll anatomy in the user-facing chooser.
- Whether newer punk-expression research can expand clothing, hair, posture,
  and color without making the avatars realistic or overly defined.
- Whether fixed registration can support small head poses and collected
  equipment without breaking garment alignment.
- Whether one assembled identity can render consistently across compact and
  expanded product contexts.

## Current QA

- The character rendering returns to the simplified classic UPRISE style.
- Facial construction is deliberately reduced and non-photorealistic.
- Seven punk expression families are represented primarily through wardrobe,
  hair, posture, and restrained accent color.
- Open outerwear preserves the undershirt graphic area.
- Bounded poses retain a shared neck, shoulder, and chest registration.
- Collected overlays include shirts, open outerwear, suspenders, buttons,
  patches, piercings, and compatible headwear.
- Feed, Home/Plot, and Personal Space render contexts are shown.
- The sheet does not prove final sprite registration, compositing, clipping,
  animation, or production export quality.

## Approval Boundary

Approval selects the visual-system direction and authorizes deterministic
production specifications as the next step. It does not approve these
generated raster examples as runtime assets.

## Delivery Correction

This sheet was created from a mistaken interpretation of "spec sheet." The
requested deliverable was a full character style sheet, not a system
architecture sheet. Do not use this file as evidence of founder approval or as
the response to the character-style request.
