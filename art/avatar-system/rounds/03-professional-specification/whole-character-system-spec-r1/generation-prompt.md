# Whole-Character Catalog System Specification R1

Date: 2026-07-24

## Purpose

Create one professional visual system sheet that shows how the selected
whole-character catalog direction can support:

- the classic simplified UPRISE avatar language;
- researched punk subcommunity expression;
- bounded personality poses;
- collected garment and accessory overlays;
- one shared character transform; and
- normalized rendering across Feed, Home/Plot, and Personal Space contexts.

This sheet is a visual architecture proposal. It is not production character
art and does not activate product behavior.

## Reference Hierarchy

1. `../../../references/legacy-avatar-boards/0_3 (1).jpeg`
   - Primary character-rendering reference.
   - Simple facial construction, bold flat silhouettes, belly-up crops, and
     ambiguous identity are the visual ceiling.
2. `../punk-subcommunity-expression-study-r2/01-punk-subcommunity-expression-study-r2.png`
   - Wardrobe, hair, expression, and accent-color research only.
   - Do not inherit its portrait realism, anatomical detail, or facial
     specificity.
3. `../../02-modular-construction-proof/classic-plot-avatar-r1/02-classic-modular-construction-proof-r2.png`
   - Technical layout, registration, layer, and crop-diagram reference only.

## Required Panels

1. Catalog Expression Families
   - Crust, Gutter, Street, 1977, Anarcho, Skate, and 1980s Day-Glo.
   - Translate each family into the simplified classic avatar style.
2. Authored Whole-Character Core
   - Fixed portrait canvas, centerline, crown line, eye line, neck anchor,
     shoulder line, chest graphic safe zone, and belly crop.
3. Bounded Personality Poses
   - Neutral front, slight left, slight right, and a small tilt/expression.
   - Keep garment and body registration stable.
4. Collected Equipment
   - Default tee, collected tee, open outerwear, suspenders, buttons, patches,
     piercings, and compatible headwear.
   - User-facing selection remains a whole character.
5. One Shared Transform
   - Character and equipped layers scale and move together.
6. Normalized Renders
   - Feed source rail, Home/Plot top shell, and Personal Space/Inventory.
7. Catalog Growth
   - Show that future authored packs can cover many music subcommunities
     without changing the shared visual language.

## Art-Direction Locks

- Mostly black, white, and warm off-white.
- One restrained accent color per example.
- Thick, slightly imperfect hand-inked lines with a light xerox texture.
- Simple, vague facial construction rather than realistic portrait detail.
- Open outerwear must preserve the undershirt graphic area.
- No photorealism, 3D rendering, cyberpunk treatment, generic app chrome, or
  real band logos.
