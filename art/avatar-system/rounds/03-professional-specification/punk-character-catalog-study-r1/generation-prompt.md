## Shared Brief

Create two matched landscape 4:3 review sheets for a whole-character UPRISE
avatar catalog. Each sheet contains exactly twelve heavily stylized punk
characters in a four-column by three-row grid.

Characters are belly-up, non-circular, and presented at a common scale. Keep
the shoulder, torso, chest-print, belly-crop, and future garment-registration
areas as consistent as possible. Restrained head turns or tilts may add
personality, but the garment frame must remain stable.

Use bold hand-inked black contours, off-white and grayscale foundations,
authored skin tones, one restrained fluorescent chartreuse accent, and subtle
xerox or screenprint texture. Keep the characters illustrated and cohesive,
not photorealistic, 3D, anime, corporate-vector, paper-doll, or extreme
caricature.

Every character should have a distinct head and face silhouette, skin tone,
hair, facial marks, accessories, and punk styling while remaining part of one
UPRISE family. Draw from overlapping classic 77, street, crust, anarcho/peace,
hardcore, skate, Oi-inspired cropped, folk-punk, post-punk/deathrock, glam,
ska-punk, and garage visual vocabulary without labeling or stereotyping the
characters.

Avoid extremist symbols, real band logos, copyrighted marks, demographic
caricature, and repeated uniform costumes. Keep outerwear open and preserve a
readable undershirt chest plane. Use generic UPRISE lightning or abstract patch
language only.
