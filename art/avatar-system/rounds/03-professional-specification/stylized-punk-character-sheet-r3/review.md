## Current QA

- The sheet contains 16 equal-scale belly-up punk characters.
- Character faces are simplified and less portrait-like than the R2
  subcommunity study.
- Characters vary in head silhouette, hair, skin tone, expression, clothing,
  and restrained accent color.
- Outer layers remain open enough to preserve the undershirt graphic area.
- Buttons, patches, piercings, suspenders, bandannas, glasses, and headwear
  appear across the cast.
- The sheet contains no system diagrams or interface material.
