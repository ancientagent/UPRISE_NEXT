# Stylized Punk Character Sheet R3

Date: 2026-07-24

## Requested Deliverable

Create one uninterrupted character style sheet containing 16 distinct,
belly-up punk avatars.

## Visual Target

- Land between the simple classic UPRISE avatar board and the stronger
  illustrated sticker references.
- Keep faces simple, ambiguous, and non-photorealistic.
- Avoid both extremes: overly defined portraits and bland repeated templates.
- Use strong hair silhouettes, varied head shapes, posture, expression,
  clothing, piercings, buttons, patches, and restrained accent color.
- Keep outer jackets, vests, and overshirts open so the undershirt graphic area
  remains visible.

## Cast Range

The 16 characters should draw from varied punk expression, including crust,
gutter, street, 1977, anarcho, peace, skate, hardcore, folk, 1980s day-glo,
post-punk/deathrock, ska/two-tone, garage, riot-grrrl, queer, and regional DIY
influences.

These are visual influences, not labels or costumes. The sheet must show
individual characters rather than one repeated face with changed hair.

## Expression Range

Include laughing, sly, bored, skeptical, defiant, calm, tired, mischievous,
joyful, irritated, deadpan, confident, friendly, intense, amused, and soft
expressions.

## Exclusions

- No system diagrams, registration guides, UI contexts, or equipment panels.
- No photorealism, semi-realistic portraits, 3D rendering, or cyberpunk tech.
- No real band names or logos.
- No closed outerwear hiding the undershirt area.
- No labels beneath individual characters.
