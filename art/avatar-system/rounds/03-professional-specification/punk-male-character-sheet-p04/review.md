# Punk Male Character Sheet P04 Review

Status: pending founder review
Date: 2026-07-26

## Review Asset

- `01-punk-male-character-sheet-p04.png`

## What Changed From P03

- Empty outlined patch placeholders were replaced by installed fictional
  band-name and logo patches.
- Hair and garment silhouettes use substantially more spikes and studs.
- Patch proportions look natural on jackets, vests, suspenders, and workwear
  rather than forcing oversized blank host panels.
- Open outerwear and visible undershirts remain intact.

## What Worked

- The sheet reads more immediately as Punk while maintaining the approved R3
  illustration balance.
- Patches demonstrate useful horizontal, square, and small-format applications
  and remain visibly separate from garment construction.
- Shoulder and lapel spikes add energy without obscuring shirt art.
- Expressions, skin tones, locs, crops, shags, quiffs, and charged styles keep
  the cast from becoming one repeated character.

## Review Risks

- Mohawk and radial-spike silhouettes dominate the upper rows; founder review
  should decide whether the energy is balanced or over-concentrated.
- Bihawk, trihawk, devilock, and rat-tail constructions are still not all
  unmistakable at this sheet crop.
- Hoop and small-ear-jewelry density remains higher than the clean production
  base should use.
- Generated patch text/logos are fixture art and require manual redraw.
- Production garments must retain clean host zones underneath removable patch
  objects.

## Approval Boundary

Founder approval may select character balance, spike/stud intensity, garment
proportions, and installed-patch presentation. It does not make this generated
sheet production-ready runtime art.
