## Founder Decision

Rejected on 2026-07-24 because the neck sockets are not uniform. The neck width,
length, and head-to-neck join vary across the generated presets. This violates
the shared-registration contract and makes the sheet unsuitable as a visual
approval target.

The head silhouettes are also too exaggerated. Several presets achieve variety
through stretched, squared, or widened proportions that read as caricature
rather than subtle choices within the selected classic avatar language.

Do not revise this result by accepting approximate neck similarity. The next
round must change the construction method so one deterministic neck/socket
master is duplicated across every preset. It must also keep every head inside a
plausible shared proportion envelope and create variety through restrained
changes to jaw, cheek, temple, chin, ear, brow, and facial-mark geometry.
