## Approval Boundary

This sheet tests whether the whole-character catalog can represent visible
niche subcommunities, outward expression, color, garment history, and
personality without flattening Punk into one costume.

This sheet may inform subcommunity clothing, hair, expression, repair, color,
and accessory vocabulary. Its portrait realism, anatomical definition, facial
specificity, and individualized casting are not approved for the UPRISE avatar
rendering language.

## Current QA

- Seven requested punk expression families are labeled.
- Each family contains two visually distinct characters.
- Fourteen characters are present.
- Garment categories, color, repair, paint, and layering vary materially across

- Facial expression is more varied than R1.
- The characters are too realistic and overly defined for the selected classic
  UPRISE avatar language.

  construction and sticker-like whole-character silhouettes.
- The sheet uses invented abstract patches and marks; no source image was
  intentionally copied.
- Some character anatomy, crops, and garment anchors differ because this is
  still generated concept art.
- A separate deterministic character-core and accessory-anchor proof remains
  required after visual approval.
