## Clarifications

- The public launch surface is an in-universe dispatch archive, not the in-app
  Feed and not a generic corporate blog.
- A single pseudonymous narrator/byline gives the dispatches continuity.
- The radio tower being erected is launch atmosphere and narrative progression;
  it must not present fabricated community activity as product data.
- The door is the handoff to normal account creation, then location/music
  community onboarding and guided camera avatar capture.
- An email-for-dispatches signup is distinct from account creation. Subscriber
  status must never silently create a listener account.
- A manila-folder packet and access phrase are narrative artifacts, not actual
  authentication credentials.
- The public pre-launch experience is a vertical illustrated descent: city and
  tower construction above ground, the tower footing/concrete threshold, then
  an underground local-music space. Earlier alley/ladder framing is superseded
  by the later continuous tower-centered camera route. This is launch
  narrative, not a Home Scene or Feed replacement.
- Launch activation has a distinct upward return animation: the completed
  tower rises from underground, the sky gains color and sunrise, and an UPRISE
  title presentation precedes Registrar. Status: design-only / deferred.
- A launch visitor may continue the desktop sequence through a QR handoff to
  a mobile guided selfie capture page. The QR is a cross-device bridge to the
  same account/onboarding session, not an authentication method. Status: open
  implementation contract.
- After selfie capture, the onboarding reveal enters the expanded Home Scene
  top shell for avatar-style selection and handle entry. Completion places the
  avatar in its normal left-side Home Scene position and resolves city/community
  context on the right. Status: proposed sequence; must agree with the
  Registrar-owned profile contract before implementation.
- Public readers may receive dispatches and progress material. Any ability to
  ask questions, comment, or otherwise interact remains open; do not imply or
  build a public discussion system from this direction alone.
- The public visual language should avoid urgency-marketing and feature sales.
  It is a dark, greenscale launch-console / transmission aesthetic, with
  UPRISE green as the active-signal accent. Status: settled visual direction.
- Candidate public modules are a public-signal status mark, mission text,
  text-mode broadcast, spoken-manifesto playback, update alerts, and a progress
  view. These are visual/content concepts only; their content owners,
  persistence, and notification behavior remain open.
- The Dispatch Index is one public content section for community/team updates,
  not the whole public launch surface. The terminal shell should also expose
  public `Blog`, `System` (how UPRISE works), and `Contact` sections in a
  concise top or right-side navigation area. These are public information
  destinations, not authenticated product modules, Feed tabs, or new runtime
  routes until their content ownership and implementation contracts are set.
- A ZIP-like passcode may be an interesting narrative/local-context entry
  control for progress or later community connection, but it cannot be called
  login or operate as authentication until its privacy, authorization, and
  destination rules are defined. Status: open founder/product decision.
- The approved public-launch visual route is one steady vertical crane descent
  centered on an unfinished radio tower. Buildings tightly frame the shot and
  provide parallax; the tower's central spine and crossbeams are the path.
  Status: settled visual direction.
- The camera reaches the physical tower footing, passes through concrete into a
  brief black/xerox-grain transition, then emerges in a compact underground
  DIY music room. Status: settled visual direction.
- The underground reveal places the viewer behind a couch, with a worn laptop
  in their lap and a tiny stage approximately ten feet away. The room is
  cramped and shadowy, not a large bunker or venue. The laptop eventually
  fills the frame. Status: settled visual direction.

- Public dispatch archive and launch narrative.
- Tower-progress illustration and eventual entry-door handoff.
- Email updates signup, pending an explicit storage/delivery owner contract.
- Narrative intake folder with map fragment/access phrase.
- Standard account creation and Home Scene onboarding after the entry door.
- Pre-launch underground descent and launch-day upward activation sequence.
- Cross-device QR handoff from a desktop launch presentation to mobile selfie
  capture, followed by avatar selection and the expanded-top-shell reveal.
- Greenscale public launch-console visual system: a secure-transmission widget,
  mission area, text/voice broadcast treatment, and restrained update signals.
- Public terminal information architecture: Dispatch Index, Blog, System, and
  Contact, with Dispatch Index as the initial visual target.
- Tower descent storyboard and eventual camera-motion prototype. Current

## Working Interpretation

- Keep the story as a breadcrumb trail: discovery, field notes, increasing
  signal, then activation.
- The initial public descent is black-and-white xerox/zine illustration with
  chartreuse reserved for active signal/light/UI emphasis. Restrained red can
  carry a meaningful annotation or route, not decorative saturation.
- The current public terminal candidate uses muted amber/olive screen type on
  a charcoal/xerox ground rather than bright UPRISE green or neon. Treat that
  as the favored terminal-screen direction unless a later approved visual
  target changes it.
- The ascent is a launch-state presentation, not an everyday navigation path.
  A normal returning-user path must stay direct once its account/launch rules
  are specified.
- "Dark web" is a visual metaphor for a stripped-down underground transmission
  surface, not a claim of hidden-network technology, anonymity, or alternate
  authentication.
- Keep real authentication conventional: Google and/or email credentials; no
  shared passphrase or fictional security mechanism.
- The first visual draft may demonstrate the signup/folder interaction without
  activating mailing-list persistence or delivery.

## Do Not Drift

- Do not make public dispatches a replacement for the UPRISE Feed.
- Do not represent the narrator as an actual clandestine organization or a
  real-world political group.
- Do not use the access phrase as an account password or authorization bypass.
- Do not imply that an email-only subscriber has entered or joined a Home
  Scene.
- Do not make the launch animation the only way a verified account can enter
  UPRISE after launch.
- Do not treat QR scanning as login, consent, selfie-capture permission, or
  cross-device session ownership without a formal security and privacy design.
- Do not invent question/comment mechanics from the public underground-space
  narrative.
- Do not use a ZIP/passcode as a substitute for real account authentication,
  nor imply it grants access to private community or account data.
- Do not turn the descent into disconnected city, alley, and room website
  sections. It is one continuous tower-centered camera route.
- Do not portray the below-ground space as a large open bunker, nor replace the
  portable laptop with a freestanding monitor.
