## Clarifications

- P01 is not rejected outright as a head-and-hair visual reference.
- Hoop earrings appear too frequently and must not read as a default avatar
  trait.
- Ear jewelry should be optional and distributed sparingly across character
  studies.
- The accessory vocabulary can include plugs, a single dangling earring with
  the other ear bare, and an ox/bull-ring-style septum.
- Accessory exploration should use one or two dedicated sheets rather than
  crowding the head-and-hair approval sheets.

## Feature Sets

- No new runtime feature is activated.
- This clarification changes asset composition and review criteria only.

## Working Interpretation

- Keep most base heads free of ear jewelry.
- Demonstrate jewelry variety through optional accessory layers, including
  plugs, a one-ear dangle, septum jewelry, and small hoops only where
  compositionally useful.
- Use neutral standard heads to demonstrate accessory fit without converting
  those preview combinations into fixed character identities.
- Do not solve repetition by replacing every hoop with the same alternate
  earring.

- Do not reject the usable parts of P01 because one accessory is overused.
- Do not make piercings mandatory for Punk avatars.
