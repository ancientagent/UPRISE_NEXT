## Clarifications

- UPRISE's ultimate purpose is to establish a viable, community-powered music industry.
- That industry is intended to become self-governing and self-sufficient through the Proof of Support protocol.
- Public explanation must immediately distinguish UPRISE from another music app or music social-media platform and give people a purpose they can rally behind.
- `Blueprints for the next DIY revolution` is the current founder-proposed rallying language.
- The system is not a financial or cryptocurrency product. Public messaging should not foreground ledgers, cryptography, blockchain, or other implementation language that could obscure or alarm people about its actual purpose.
- Type: settled platform-purpose clarification; governance, funding, and ownership mechanisms remain open/deferred until their owner contracts are approved.
- Likely owner: platform orientation and identity doctrine, with the protocol architecture owned by `docs/specs/engagement/support-and-participation.md` and future governance/economic owner sections.

## Feature Sets

- Proof of Support as the enabling protocol
- Raw basis: "self governing and self sufficent through the use of proof of support protocol"
- Included behavior:
  - Support, verified action, Participation, influence evidence, and future Trusted Role qualification operate as related parts of one protocol.
  - The protocol is intended to make community backing credible, participation auditable, and future stewardship accountable.
  - The mature platform direction is a community-powered music industry capable of sustaining and governing itself.
- Excluded / not activated:
  - Immediate council or governance runtime.
  - Automatic authority from Support balances, Support taps, money spent, or collectible ownership.
  - Unapproved treasury, legal-ownership, token, equity, payout, or decentralization mechanics.
  - Any change to Fair Play, voting, propagation, or artist ranking.
- Status: settled platform purpose; implementation mechanics remain staged, open, or deferred according to their owner contracts.

## Working Interpretation

- The Proof of Support protocol is not merely a Feed reaction or event-response feature. It is the connective protocol intended to turn credible community backing and verified participation into a sustainable local music ecosystem.
- `Self-governing` and `self-sufficient` describe the mature destination, not an authorization to invent governance or financial mechanisms now.
- The city-tier music platform must still function independently while the Proof of Support protocol is developed in later stages.
- A useful communication hierarchy is: the community-powered music industry is the category/purpose; the next DIY revolution is the rallying frame; Proof of Support is the enabling protocol rather than the opening public pitch.

## Do Not Drift

- Do not reduce the Proof of Support protocol to a reaction button, event RSVP replacement, loyalty program, or isolated points feature.
- Do not separate Support/Participation mechanics from the ultimate goal of a viable, community-powered music industry.
- Do not market UPRISE as another music app, streaming service, or music social network.
- Do not lead public messaging with cryptography, ledgers, protocol mechanics, or financial-system comparisons.
- Do not treat the long-term self-governance direction as current permission to invent councils, weighted authority, financial custody, legal ownership, or decentralization mechanics.
- Do not let Support or Participation buy Fair Play placement, voting power, propagation, or artist rank.
