## Clarifications

- The requested design spec should cover a photo-guided, highly stylized
  listener-avatar generator.
- The avatar's visual direction should reflect the listener's music-community
  context without changing or stereotyping personal identity.
- The spec must explain how custom buttons, patches, and related wearables
  attach to the listener's wardrobe and visually promote a source.
- Google Flow is the current visual-generation experiment.
- The avatar maker should produce the user's stylized identity in the standard
  UPRISE tee.
- Clothing selection happens separately after avatar generation.
- The avatar-maker explanation is complete for this design package. Remaining
  work should focus on wardrobe selection, wearable attachment, and promotion.
- Participation-based wardrobe unlocking is a founder idea, not yet a settled
  currency, threshold, permanence, or acquisition rule.
- Generator ideas are requested, but proposed behavior must remain clearly
  separated from settled product rules.

## Feature Sets

- Photo-guided core UPRISE avatar creation.
- Standard UPRISE tee as the generated starter garment.
- Stable illustrated identity plus separately selected modular wardrobe.
- Optional music-community wardrobe collections.
- Personal Space/Inventory wearable equipping.
- Source-owned promotional buttons, patches, pins, and shirts.
- Compact versus expanded wearable visibility.
- Potential Participation progression for UPRISE/community wardrobe options.

## Working Interpretation

- Use Google Flow for private art exploration and iterative references.
- Do not assume Flow's private `@me` avatar can be integrated into UPRISE.
- Recommend a hybrid system: AI generates a normalized stylized identity in
  the standard UPRISE tee; the tee is separated as the starter top, while later
  UPRISE garments and digital-merch objects remain deterministic and
  listener-selected.
- Treat the expanded centered top-shell avatar as the primary readable patch
  presentation.
- Keep Feed publication, Support, analytics, click destinations, ownership,
  payments, and source-authoring mechanics unresolved until owner specs decide
  them.
- Keep UPRISE/community wardrobe progression separate from unauthorized access
  to source-owned band merch.

## Do Not Drift

- Do not treat Google Flow as an available UPRISE API without verification.
- Do not flatten permanent shirts, outerwear, buttons, or patches into the
  generated identity.
- Do not automatically dress the listener according to a music-community
  stereotype.
- Do not infer music community, politics, demographic identity, or personality
  from a photo.
- Do not activate photo storage, public generation, promotions, analytics,
  payments, or marketplace behavior from this design request.
- Do not treat Participation as spendable currency or promise permanent
  wardrobe unlocks without an owner-spec decision.
