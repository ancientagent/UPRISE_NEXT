  1. male-presenting;
  2. female-presenting;
  3. nonbinary.

  than treating hairstyles as isolated costume pieces.

  Punk hairstyle vocabulary.

  source asset.

## Working Interpretation

- The mixed P01 head sheet is retained as a revision reference.
- The replacement male-presenting pack should use a source-backed 20-style
  roster expanded to 24 styles and the approved R3 illustration balance.
- Female-presenting and nonbinary research/rosters should be prepared
  separately rather than produced as palette-swapped versions of the male pack.
- Hair, expression, and subcommunity identity must remain related but not
  stereotypically locked together.
- Hair color should use a separate authored palette/mask where technically
  feasible so color does not require duplicate geometry.
- A hair-color overview sheet may establish solid colors, bleached treatments,
  visible roots, tips, split dye, and limited multicolor treatments for
  approval, but it does not define a separate runtime asset family.
- In the layered source asset, alternate solid colors and special color
  treatments can be authored as selectable color layers clipped to the
  approved hairstyle silhouette.
- Length, thickness, arrangement, and silhouette changes should remain
  authored geometry variants rather than unrestricted runtime scaling.
- Scalp tattoos should be authored as optional overlays compatible with
  shaved, buzzed, and other sufficiently exposed-scalp hairstyles.
